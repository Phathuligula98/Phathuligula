Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order?

We recommend ordering at least 30 minutes before your desired pickup time. For larger orders (10+ items), please order 2-3 hours in advance.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, you can cancel up to 15 minutes before your pickup time. Contact us directly via WhatsApp or phone.

Do you cater for dietary requirements?

Absolutely! We offer vegetarian, vegan, and halal options. Please mention any allergies or dietary needs in the special requests box.

How do I pay?

We accept cash, SnapScan, Zapper, and all major bank cards. You can also pay via EFT - we'll send details with your order confirmation.
