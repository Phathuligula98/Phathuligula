About Mzansi Bite

Our story, mission, and the people behind the flavour

From Chef's Dream to Food Truck Reality

In 2023, Chef Thabo Nkosi made a bold decision. After 15 years working in corporate kitchens and five-star hotels, he traded his chef's whites for an apron and opened the first Mzansi Bite food truck at the Bryanston Organic Market.

"I wanted to bring the flavours I grew up with to the streets," says Thabo. "South Africa has such incredible culinary diversity, but our street food scene wasn't reflecting that. I wanted to change that."

What started as a single truck quickly gained a loyal following. Social media buzzed about the "rainbow bunny chow" and the "smoked boerewos roll with chakalaka sauce." Within a year, Mzansi Bite expanded to three trucks, serving hundreds of customers daily across Johannesburg and Pretoria.

Today, Mzansi Bite is more than a food truck—it's a celebration of South African culture, community, and cuisine.

Our Mission

To celebrate South Africa's diverse culinary heritage by offering innovative, high-quality street food that is accessible, affordable, and authentic, while supporting local farmers and suppliers.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's leading gourmet food truck brand, expanding to five major cities by 2028, while maintaining our commitment to quality, community, and sustainability.

Our Core Values

Community

We believe in building strong relationships with our customers, suppliers, and the communities we serve.

Sustainability

We source locally, reduce waste, and use eco-friendly packaging whenever possible.

Authenticity

Every dish we serve is true to its South African roots, made with traditional techniques and fresh ingredients.

Quality

We never compromise on quality. Every meal that leaves our trucks is prepared with care and excellence.

Giving Back to Our Community

At Mzansi Bite, we believe in feeding more than just appetites. Here's how we give back:

Weekly Meal Donations

Every Friday, we donate 50 meals to local shelters and orphanages in Johannesburg.

Youth Culinary Workshops

We host free cooking workshops for youth in underserved communities, teaching basic culinary skills.

Supporting Local Farmers

We source 80% of our ingredients from local South African farmers, supporting the agricultural sector.

Green Initiative

For every 100 meals sold, we plant a tree in partnership with Food & Trees for Africa.
